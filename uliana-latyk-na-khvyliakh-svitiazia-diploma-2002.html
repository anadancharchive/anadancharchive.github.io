Description

This diploma records Латик Уляна as the recipient of the II Prize at the Festival of Ukrainian Pop Song «На хвилях Світязя!».

The document identifies the recipient as being from Львів and is dated 7 July 2002 in Луцьк.

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the early artistic career of the singer under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The diploma records the recipient as Латик Уляна. The standardized Latin-script form Uliana Latyk, the later professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document directly records Uliana Latyk as the recipient of the II Prize at the Festival of Ukrainian Pop Song «На хвилях Світязя!». It identifies her as being from Lviv and is dated 7 July 2002 in Lutsk. The handwritten signatures of the jury chair and organizing committee chair are not identified in this archival entry.
