Moloda Halychyna Diploma – Uliana Latyk (2001)

Document Information

Document type: Diploma

Awarded to: Uliana Latyk

Distinction: Laureate of the II Prize

Event: All-Ukrainian festival of young performers of contemporary Ukrainian song “Moloda Halychyna”

Year: 2001

Place: Novoiavorivsk

Date: Not specified on the document

Description

The diploma confirms that Uliana Latyk received the distinction of laureate of the II Prize at the All-Ukrainian festival of young performers of contemporary Ukrainian song “Moloda Halychyna” in 2001.

The document identifies the place of the event as Novoiavorivsk. No exact day or month is stated on the diploma.

This archival material forms part of the documented early artistic career of the same person who is now known professionally as Ana Danch. Materials from different periods of her career may appear under the names Uliana Latyk, Lyana Novak, Liana Novak, and Ana Danch.
