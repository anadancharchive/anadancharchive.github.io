Description

On 2 March 2016, Ukrainian media outlet Дивись.info published an article about the team of experts assembled for the social project «Міс Львів».

The article identifies Ляна Новак as a singer, television presenter and former lead vocalist of the group TRISHI. It also documents her professional and public activities at the time, including her work as a television presenter, president of a charitable foundation and founder of the Ball of Flowers in Ukraine.

The publication is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an independent media record documenting the professional name Lyana Novak during an earlier period in the career of the artist now known professionally as Ana Danch.
