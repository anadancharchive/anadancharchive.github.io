Description

“Ой у вишневому саду” (Oi u vyshnevomu sadu) is a Ukrainian traditional folk song that tells a tender story of love unfolding in the symbolic setting of a cherry orchard. The song conveys deep emotions through poetic images of nature, intertwining them with the inner world of a person. Its gentle romantic atmosphere reflects sincerity, emotional depth and the distinctive spirit of Ukrainian folk tradition.
