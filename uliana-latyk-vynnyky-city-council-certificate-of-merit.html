Description

This certificate of merit records Уляна Латик, a student of 11-а класу середньої школи №29, as a winner of regional, All-Ukrainian, and international festivals and competitions of pop song.

The certificate was awarded by the Винниківська міська рада Львівської області on the occasion of the celebration of Дня міста Винники.

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the early artistic career of the singer under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The document records the recipient as Уляна Латик. The standardized Latin-script form Uliana Latyk, the later professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document directly records Uliana Latyk as a student of 11-а класу of secondary school №29 and as a winner of regional, All-Ukrainian, and international festivals and competitions of pop song. It was awarded by the Vynnyky City Council of Lviv Oblast on the occasion of the celebration of Vynnyky City Day and identifies С.І. Уваров as the mayor of Vynnyky. The visible document does not state an exact date or year, so these details are not added to this archival entry.
