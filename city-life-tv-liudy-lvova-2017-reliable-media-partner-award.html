Description

This award plaque records City Life TV as a «Надійний медіа-партнер» of «Люди Львова-2017».

The award was presented by RIA Львів.

The document itself identifies the recipient as City Life TV. The names Ana Danch, Lyana Novak, and Liana Novak do not appear on the visible award plaque.

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as archival material related to the documented artistic and media career of Ana Danch.
