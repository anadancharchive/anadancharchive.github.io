Description

“Вітер надій” (Wind of Hope) is a song from Ana Danch’s early artistic period. It was written in 2000 by guitarist, composer, arranger and lead vocalist of the rock band Galaktika, Viacheslav Sinchuk, specifically for the young singer Uliana Latyk. The song explores love, dreams and hopes - the essential emotions that give meaning to human life. After remaining unreleased for 24 years, the song was given a contemporary arrangement and finally released to the world, allowing this piece from Ana Danch’s early creative history to find its audience.
