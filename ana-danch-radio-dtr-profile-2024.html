Description

Radio DTR published the biographical article “Uliana Danchul powraca na scenę – historia artystki, która nie zapomniała o swojej pasji” on 5 November 2024. The publication presents an overview of the artist’s musical background and her return to creative activity.

The article directly identifies Uliana Danchul as being widely known under the stage name Ana Danch and describes her as a Ukrainian singer and songwriter. It states that she was born in Lviv in 1987 and began her involvement with music at the age of seven.

Radio DTR discusses the artist’s participation in Ukrainian and international music competitions and festivals and refers to her earlier activity as a lead vocalist in Ukrainian music groups. The publication also describes a long interruption in her artistic career followed by her return to the stage.

The article mentions several elements of her early artistic biography, including the festivals “Piosenny Vernisaż”, “Młoda Galyczyna” and “Azowskie Parusy”, the song “Nie płacz”, and the groups referred to in the Polish-language publication as “Deser” and “Trisza”.

The publication further states that in October 2024 Ana Danch announced two new songs and the beginning of work on an album, presenting this activity as part of her renewed musical career.
