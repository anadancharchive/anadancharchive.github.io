Description

This diploma records ЛАТИК УЛЯНІ in connection with the VI регіональний дитячий і молодіжний фестиваль української естрадної пісні «Цвіт папороті».

The document identifies the nomination as «Популярна пісня» and the age category as середня вікова група.

The festival document displays Стрий as part of its printed festival identification.

The document itself records the recipient under the surname and given name ЛАТИК УЛЯНІ. The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible diploma.

Notes

The diploma directly records ЛАТИК УЛЯНІ in the nomination «Популярна пісня» for the середня вікова група at the VI regional children's and youth festival of Ukrainian pop song «Цвіт папороті».

The standardized Latin-script birth name Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk, the professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

No year or exact date is stated on the visible diploma. The designation VI identifies the edition of the festival and is not used to infer a date.

No award placement or laureate status is stated on the visible diploma, so none is assigned in this archival entry. Handwritten signatures are not independently interpreted.
