Description

Minatrix.FM features an artist profile titled “Ana Danch - Biography and discography, all albums and songs”, dedicated to Ukrainian singer and songwriter Ana Danch.

The profile identifies Ana Danch as a Ukrainian singer and songwriter from Lviv and states that her real name is Ulyana Myroslavivna Danchul, with the maiden name Latyk. It gives her date of birth as 16 November 1987 and describes the beginning of her musical activity in childhood, including vocal lessons from the age of seven and her first stage appearance at the age of nine.

The biography documents her participation in numerous national and international vocal competitions and festivals, including Song Vernissage, On the Waves of Svityaz, Chervona Ruta, Black Sea Games, Azov Sails, Young Galicia, Hope, Musical Toloka, Crystal Lark, New Song in Old Krakow and others.

The profile also describes her education, her work as lead vocalist of the groups Desert in 2005–2006 and Tryshi in 2006–2007, as well as her later work in television and production. It mentions Ukrainian Fashion Channel, City Life TV, and the television programs “Life by Liana” and “Children and the City”.

The source states that in 2021 the artist resumed her active music career under the stage name Ana Danch. The page also includes a dedicated music section presenting recordings and releases associated with Ana Danch on Minatrix.FM.

Notes

The Minatrix.FM profile directly identifies Ana Danch as Ulyana Myroslavivna Danchul and states that her maiden name is Latyk.

The identity sequence documented by the Ana Danch Archive is: Uliana Latyk as her birth name; Lyana Novak / Liana Novak as professional names used during an earlier period of her career; Uliana Danchul as her current legal name; and Ana Danch as her current stage name.

The source is presented by Minatrix.FM within its Artists section and combines a biographical profile with a catalogue of music associated with Ana Danch.

No publication date is displayed on the source page. Dates shown beside individual recordings on the page relate to those music entries and are not treated by the Ana Danch Archive as the publication date of the artist profile.

The source page does not display an individual author for the biography, so the Ana Danch Archive does not assign individual authorship without direct evidence.

One screenshot of the online artist profile is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive together with the original source reference.
