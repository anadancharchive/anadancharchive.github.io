Description

Os Garotos de Liverpool published the article “Ana Danch nos transporta pela atmosfera de verão em 'La Isla Bonita', faixa cantada em inglês e espanhol” on 6 August 2026. Written by Linna Santiago, the Portuguese-language publication introduces Ukrainian singer-songwriter Ana Danch to the website and focuses on her single “La Isla Bonita”.

The article states that “La Isla Bonita” was released during the second half of July and highlights its melodic and soft vocal lines, string arrangements, engaging beats and light atmosphere. The publication places the recording within contemporary pop and indie music.

Os Garotos de Liverpool emphasizes that the song is performed in English and Spanish. According to the article, it tells a story about the feeling of freedom experienced during an unforgettable summer night by the sea, following characters who embrace adventure while exploring an abandoned house, diving into the sea and sharing moments that remain in their memories.

The publication also interprets the song as a celebration of youth, spontaneity and the desire to escape from routine, connecting its musical atmosphere with themes of emotion, adventure and freedom.

The article states that the composition was created by Ana Danch herself and describes its warm melodies as part of the artist’s approach to creating music capable of transporting listeners into emotionally vivid settings.
