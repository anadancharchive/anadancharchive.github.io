Description

"Світ таємних мрій" is an original pop song about love, dreams and the power of sincere feelings. Performed by Ana Danch, with lyrics written by Uliana Danchul and music composed by Mykhailo Bushko, it was released as a digital single on 12 May 2024, inviting listeners into a romantic world where love and imagination come together.
