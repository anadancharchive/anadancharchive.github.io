Description

This award statuette records Ляна Новак as «Найкрасивіша співачка» (“Most Beautiful Singer”).

The base of the statuette bears the title «Перевірка на вечірку», a television programme that aired on the ZIK television channel.

The award is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the artistic career of the singer during the period when she performed professionally under the name Lyana Novak.

Notes

The award directly identifies Ляна Новак and bears the recognition «Найкрасивіша співачка». The title «Перевірка на вечірку» appears on the base of the statuette.

The standardized Latin-script form Lyana Novak, the alternative professional form Liana Novak, the birth name Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context. No date or year is stated on the visible award, so no date is assigned to this archival entry.
