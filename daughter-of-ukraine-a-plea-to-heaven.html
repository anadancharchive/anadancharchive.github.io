Description

"Daughter of Ukraine (A Plea to Heaven)" is an original pop and pop-rock single by Ana Danch. Written in response to the ongoing war in Ukraine, the song combines emotional vocals with a message of hope, resilience, and peace. Conceived as a musical prayer and a tribute to the strength of the Ukrainian people, it reflects the desire for justice, freedom, and a peaceful future. Released digitally on 24 February 2025 via ANA DANCH and distributed worldwide by Symphonic Distribution.
