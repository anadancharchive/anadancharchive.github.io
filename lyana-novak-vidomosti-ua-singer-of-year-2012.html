Description

In its issue № 8 (596), dated 23–29 February 2012, Відомості.UA published the article «Ляна Новак стала співачкою року» in the section «Світське життя».

The publication reports that Ляна Новак received the title «Співачка року» at the «Золотий Лев — Люди Львова-2011» awards ceremony in Lviv.

The article also states that 2011 marked the beginning of the solo project «Ляна Новак». It documents work on the album «У ритмі миру», cooperation with DJ Sender, and mentions the music videos «Не плач», «У ритмі миру» and «Балі».

The publication is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an independent contemporary media record documenting the professional name Lyana Novak during an earlier period in the career of the artist now known professionally as Ana Danch.

Notes

The original publication uses the Ukrainian name Ляна Новак. The Latin-script form Lyana Novak, as well as the later legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul and current stage name Ana Danch, are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The newspaper issue is dated 23–29 February 2012. Because the source provides a weekly publication period rather than a specific publication day for this article, no single-day publication date is assigned in this archival record.
