Description

This diploma records Уляна Латик as a participant in the festival for children and youth «Різдвяні канікули».

The document states that the diploma was awarded за участь у фестивалі and includes the handwritten institutional identification Винниківський будинок школяра.

The diploma identifies Управління освіти Львівської облдержадміністрації and Центр творчості дітей та юнацтва Галичини at the top of the document.

The document itself records the recipient under the name Уляна Латик. The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible diploma.

Notes

The diploma directly records Уляна Латик as a participant in the festival «Різдвяні канікули» and states that it was awarded for participation in the festival.

The standardized Latin-script birth name Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk, the professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document records Львів and the year 2000. No exact date is stated on the visible diploma, so no exact date is assigned in this archival entry.

The diploma documents participation in the festival and does not state a competitive placement or laureate status. No such status is assigned in this archival entry.
