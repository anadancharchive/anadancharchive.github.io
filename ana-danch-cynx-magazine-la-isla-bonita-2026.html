Description

CYNX Magazine published the feature “Ana Danch Captures One Unforgettable Summer Night on Bilingual Single ‘La Isla Bonita’” on 3 August 2026. Written by Ke, the article focuses on Ukrainian singer-songwriter Ana Danch and her bilingual single “La Isla Bonita”.

The publication states that “La Isla Bonita” was released on 16 July 2026 and was written and composed by Ana Danch. It highlights the use of English and Spanish throughout the song and describes its narrative as a summer story shaped by freedom, attraction, adventure and spontaneity.

CYNX Magazine describes the song’s characters exploring an abandoned house, leaving graffiti on its walls, diving into the sea and sharing a day they know they will remember. The article interprets the island in the song less as a specific destination and more as an emotional escape from ordinary life.

The publication also discusses the song’s warm melodies, dreamy production and bilingual storytelling, presenting these elements as part of its cinematic summer atmosphere and international character.

The article includes a biographical overview of Ana Danch, identifying her as Uliana Danchul, née Latyk, born in Lviv, Ukraine. It states that she began studying vocals at the age of seven, gave her first public performance at nine and later participated in 59 national and international vocal competitions.

CYNX Magazine also refers to Ana Danch writing “Не плач” at the age of fourteen, her work as lead vocalist of Десерт (Dessert) from 2005 to 2006 and ТРИШИ (TRISHI) from 2006 to 2007, and her return to artistic activity in 2021 under the stage name Ana Danch.
