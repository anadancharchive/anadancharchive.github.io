Description

Antennaweb Radio published the article “Ana Danch – ‘Champagne’” on 10 July 2024. The publication presents Ana Danch and focuses on her song “Champagne”.

The article introduces the release and also provides a short overview of Ana Danch’s artistic background. It refers to her origins in Lviv, the beginning of her vocal studies at the age of seven, and her participation in music competitions and festivals.

The publication also discusses elements of her earlier career, including the song “Не плач”, her musical education, and her activity in the Ukrainian groups Dessert and Trishi.

According to Antennaweb Radio, Ana Danch resumed her creative activity in 2021 under the stage name Ana Danch. The article therefore combines coverage of “Champagne” with biographical information about the artist and her return to music.
