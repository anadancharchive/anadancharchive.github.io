Description

The International Cultural Portal “Експеримент” published the article “Ana Danch презентувала англо-іспанський сингл «La Isla Bonita»” on 17 July 2026. The publication focuses on Ukrainian singer and songwriter Ana Danch and her single “La Isla Bonita”.

The article describes “La Isla Bonita” as an atmospheric composition combining English and Spanish lyrics with contemporary pop elements and a cinematic mood. It highlights the song’s dreamy guitars, rhythms influenced by the 1980s and layered vocal arrangement.

The publication presents the song as a story of freedom, emotion, adventure and an unforgettable summer night by the sea. According to the article, the narrative follows its characters as they explore an abandoned house, leave graffiti on its walls, dive into the sea and experience a day that remains in their memory.

The publication identifies Ana Danch as the author of the music and lyrics and states that “La Isla Bonita” is her first author's composition created in English and Spanish.

The article also refers to international music commentary concerning the cinematic character, atmosphere, multilingual approach and vocal delivery of the recording. These assessments are documented here as statements presented by the source.
