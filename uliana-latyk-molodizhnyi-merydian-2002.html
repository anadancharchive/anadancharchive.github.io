Description

On 23–29 May 2002, the Ukrainian newspaper Molodizhnyi Merydian (Молодіжний меридіан) published an interview with Uliana Latyk titled "Я ніколи не прагнула миттєвої слави" ("I Never Sought Instant Fame"). In the interview, Uliana reflects on the importance of developing an artistic career gradually, speaking about her early performances, vocal education, participation in festivals, creative inspirations, her first original song "Не плач", and the support of her family and audience. The publication documents Uliana Latyk's artistic development and creative views before adopting the stage name Ana Danch.
