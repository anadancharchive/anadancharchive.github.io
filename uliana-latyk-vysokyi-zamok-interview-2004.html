Description

On 31 August 2004, the Ukrainian newspaper Vysokyi Zamok (Високий Замок) published an interview by Svitlana Rovenchak with Uliana Latyk titled "Уляна Латик присвятила своєму хлопцеві пісню «Ти втратив мене»" ("Uliana Latyk Dedicated the Song "You Lost Me" to Her Boyfriend"). The interview documents an important stage in the early artistic career of Uliana Latyk, who was preparing the release of her debut album and continuing to establish herself as a young vocalist. The article discusses her musical journey, vocal education, festival achievements, original songwriting, admission to the Kyiv National University of Culture and Arts, and her future artistic ambitions. It also highlights her original song "Ty Vtratyv Mene" ("You Lost Me"), with lyrics written by Uliana Latyk and music composed by Andrii Bakun. Today, this publication remains an important archival source documenting the beginning of her professional artistic career before adopting the stage name Ana Danch.
