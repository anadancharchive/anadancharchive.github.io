Description

This diploma records Уляна Латик as a лауреат I премії of the festival «На крилах дитинства», identified on the document as a Фестиваль дитячої української естрадної пісні.

The document identifies the jury chair as М. Шуневич and prints his title as Заслужений артист України. External biographical information identifies him as Ukrainian singer Мар'ян Васильович Шуневич.

No exact date, year, or location is stated on the visible document. These details are therefore not inferred from other diplomas associated with the same festival.

The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible diploma and are provided by the archive as identity context.
