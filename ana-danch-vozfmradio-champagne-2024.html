Description

Voz FM Radio published the article “Champagne de la cantautora Ana Danch ya suena en vozfmradio” on 13 August 2024. The publication focuses on Ukrainian singer and songwriter Ana Danch and her song “Champagne”.

The title of the publication states that “Champagne” was being played on Voz FM Radio, documenting international radio and online media attention to the song in August 2024.

The article also contains a biographical overview of Ana Danch’s artistic background, including references to her early involvement with music and her activity in Ukrainian music groups. Several details in this biographical section contain editorial inaccuracies or imprecise chronology and therefore have not been reproduced as established biographical facts in this archival record.

Notes

The publication documents online and radio coverage of Ana Danch and the song “Champagne” by Voz FM Radio in August 2024.

The publication contains several editorial inaccuracies in its biographical description of Ana Danch. These statements have not been reproduced as factual biographical information in this archival record where they conflict with independently documented information preserved in the Ana Danch Archive.

Among these inconsistencies are inaccuracies concerning the chronology of the artist’s early career, the presentation and naming of her participation in Ukrainian music groups, and some descriptions of her musical projects. These details are retained only as part of the original source publication and should be understood as editorial inaccuracies of Voz FM Radio.

The statement that “Champagne” was being played on Voz FM Radio reflects the wording of the original publication and is documented here as a claim made by the source.
