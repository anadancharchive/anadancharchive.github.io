Description

This diploma records Уляна Латик as a laureate of the I Degree at the 1st All-Ukrainian Youth Festival of Arts «Музична толока».

The document records the nomination as естрадний вокал and the age-group designation as старша в.г.. It identifies the recipient as being from Львів and records Рівне, 2003.

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the early artistic career of the singer under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The diploma records the recipient as Уляна Латик. The standardized Latin-script form Uliana Latyk, the later professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document directly records Uliana Latyk as a laureate of the I Degree at the 1st All-Ukrainian Youth Festival of Arts «Музична толока» in the nomination «естрадний вокал». It identifies her as being from Lviv and records Rivne, 2003. The age-group designation is preserved as written on the document: «старша в.г.». No exact date is stated on the visible diploma, so only the year 2003 is recorded in this archival entry. The handwritten names and signatures of the jury members are not identified in this archival entry.
