Description

"Summer Ecstasy" is an original English-language house single by Ana Danch. Created through an international collaboration between songwriters from Ukraine, North Macedonia, and the United States, the song blends energetic electronic production with house rhythms and a vibrant summer atmosphere. Inspired by memories of a past summer romance, it reflects longing, nostalgia, and the hope of reliving unforgettable moments together. Released digitally on 28 June 2024 via ANA DANCH and distributed worldwide by Symphonic Distribution.
