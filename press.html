Press

The Press section documents published materials related to the artistic career of Ana Danch. Publications documenting earlier periods of her career may appear under her birth name, Uliana Latyk, or under the professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak. These materials form part of the documented artistic history of the same person now performing under the stage name Ana Danch.

Contents

The archive documents newspapers, magazines, interviews, reviews, announcements, and other editorial publications. Each publication is presented as an individual archival record with descriptive information and available source details.
