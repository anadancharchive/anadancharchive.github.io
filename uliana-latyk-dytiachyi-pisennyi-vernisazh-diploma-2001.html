Year 2001
Language Ukrainian

Description

This diploma records Уляна Латик as a laureate of the I Prize of the Second All-Ukrainian Festival of Contemporary Ukrainian Pop Song «Дитячий пісенний вернісаж – 2001» in 2001.

The document identifies Анатолій Авдієвський as the chairman of the jury.

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the early artistic career of the singer under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The diploma records the recipient as Уляна Латик. The standardized Latin-script form Uliana Latyk, the later professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document directly records Uliana Latyk as a laureate of the I Prize of the Second All-Ukrainian Festival of Contemporary Ukrainian Pop Song «Дитячий пісенний вернісаж – 2001» in 2001. The document identifies Анатолій Авдієвський as the chairman of the jury. No exact date or place of the event is stated on the visible diploma, so neither is added to this archival entry.
