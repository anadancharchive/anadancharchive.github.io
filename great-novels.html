Description

"Great Novels" is an original indie pop and dream pop single by Ana Danch. Written and composed by Uliana Danchul, the song explores inner turning points and the life stories that stay with us long after they happen. Its core message is simple: do not hesitate, love deeply, move forward, and take action. Released on 16 January 2026 via ANA DANCH and distributed worldwide by Symphonic Distribution.
