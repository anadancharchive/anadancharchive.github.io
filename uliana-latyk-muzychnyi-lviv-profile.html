Description

The Ukrainian music website Muzychnyi Lviv (Музичний Львів) published a biographical artist profile dedicated to Uliana Latyk, who later adopted the stage name Ana Danch.

The profile documents her early artistic career, including her childhood involvement in ballroom dancing, transition to singing, early stage performances, and participation in numerous Ukrainian music festivals and competitions. The publication mentions "Pisennyi Vernisazh", "Moloda Halychyna", "Azovski Vitryla", "Na Krylakh Dytynstva", "Kryshtalevyi Zhaivir", "Na Khvyliakh Svitiazia", and "Chornomorski Ihry" among her festival achievements.

The profile records that in 2001 Uliana Latyk received the "Discovery of the Year" award from the project "Zoriana Zabava" and appeared in the musical film "Новорічні пригоди у старовинному місті". It also documents her radio debut with the song "Stezhka do Shchastia" ("Path to Happiness").

The publication provides important authorship information for the song "Ne Plach" ("Don't Cry"), identifying Uliana Latyk as the lyricist and Andrii Bakun as the composer. It also recalls her performance of "Rusalonka", set to music by Ukrainian composer Ihor Bilozir, at a memorial concert dedicated to him.

The profile combines biographical information with personal statements by Uliana Latyk and concludes with a message addressed directly to the listeners and supporters of her music.
