Description

V13 Media published the article “Ana Danch Releases New Single ‘La Isla Bonita’” on 18 July 2026. Written by Aaron Willschick, the publication focuses on Ana Danch and the release of her single “La Isla Bonita”.

The article describes “La Isla Bonita” as a song written in English and Spanish. It presents the composition as a story of freedom, emotion, adventure and an unforgettable summer night by the sea.

According to the publication, the song’s characters explore an abandoned house, leave graffiti on its walls, dive into the sea and experience a day that remains in their memory.

The article also provides a biographical overview of Ana Danch, identifying her as a Ukrainian singer and songwriter working across pop, folk and electronic music. It refers to the beginning of her vocal studies at the age of seven, her first public performance at the age of nine, and participation in 59 national and international vocal competitions.

V13 Media also mentions Ana Danch’s first author's song “Не плач”, her activity as lead vocalist of the Ukrainian girl group Десерт (Dessert) from 2005 to 2006 and ТРИШИ (TRISHI) from 2006 to 2007, and her return to artistic activity in 2021 under the stage name Ana Danch.
