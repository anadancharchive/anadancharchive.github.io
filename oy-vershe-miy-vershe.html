Description

"Ой, верше, мій верше" ("Oy Vershe, Miy Vershe") is Ana Danch's interpretation of one of the best-known Lemko folk songs and one of the most celebrated Ukrainian folk songs of the twentieth century. Based on traditional music and lyrics, Ana Danch's version preserves the emotional depth and authenticity of the original while presenting this timeless Lemko classic through her own vocal style and contemporary production.
