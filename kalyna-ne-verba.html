Description

"Калина не верба" ("Kalyna Ne Verba") is Ana Danch's own adaptation of the Ukrainian traditional folk song "А калина — не верба". Based on traditional music with updated lyrics by Uliana Danchul, this version preserves the emotional depth and authenticity of the original while presenting the timeless Ukrainian folk classic through Ana Danch's own vocal style and contemporary production. Released digitally on 17 October 2025 via ANA DANCH and distributed by Symphonic Distribution.
