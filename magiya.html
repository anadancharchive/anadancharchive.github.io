Description

"Магія" (Magic) is an author's song by Ana Danch, written and composed by Uliana Danchul. The song is about a love that needs no explanation. Beneath a star-filled sky, surrounded by moonlight and a blooming garden, two people realize that they have found a kindred soul in each other.
