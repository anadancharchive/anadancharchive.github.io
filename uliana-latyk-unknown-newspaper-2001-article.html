Description

This newspaper article features Uliana Latyk during the early stage of her artistic career. Written by journalist Yana Kochan (Яна Кочан), the publication presents the young singer through a series of questions and answers, focusing on her musical achievements, artistic aspirations, and personal impressions of performing on stage. The article mentions Uliana's studies at the Vynnyky House of Schoolchildren, her participation in "Martyn Choir" under the direction of Marta Lozynska, and her success at numerous regional, national, and international vocal competitions and festivals. The publication states that Uliana Latyk was recognised as "Discovery of the Year" at the project "Zoryana Zabava by Zakhar", a fact independently confirmed in her published biography available through Music Lviv. Today, this publication remains one of the earliest preserved newspaper profiles documenting the artistic career of Uliana Latyk before adopting the stage name Ana Danch.
