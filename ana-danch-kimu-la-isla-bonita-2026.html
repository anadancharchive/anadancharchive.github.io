Description

KIMU published the feature “Ana Danch captures the freedom and romance of an unforgettable summer night on cinematic new single, La Isla Bonita” on 15 July 2026. The publication focuses on Ana Danch and her single “La Isla Bonita”.

The article presents an editorial review of the recording, highlighting its atmospheric and cinematic character, multilingual approach, vocal development and summer setting. KIMU describes the song as an immersive musical narrative built around themes of freedom, emotion, adventure and an unforgettable summer night by the sea.

The publication also provides a short biographical overview of Ana Danch. It identifies her as a Ukrainian singer-songwriter born in Lviv and refers to the beginning of her vocal studies at the age of seven, her stage debut at nine, participation in 59 national and international vocal competitions, her first author's song at the age of fourteen, and her earlier activity with the Ukrainian groups Dessert and TRISHI.

KIMU further states that 2021 marked the artist’s return to music under the name Ana Danch, directly connecting her earlier artistic history with her current stage identity.

The publication states that KIMU added “La Isla Bonita” to its New Music Spotlight and TIMELESS playlists. It also refers to other recordings from Ana Danch’s discography, including “Champagne” and “Take Me To New York”.
