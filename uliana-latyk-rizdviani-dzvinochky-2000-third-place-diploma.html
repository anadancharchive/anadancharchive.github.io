Description

This diploma records Уляна Латик as a лауреат of the festival «Різдвяні дзвіночки – 2000».

The award page directly states that she received III місце in the середня група.

The document therefore provides a direct archival record of Uliana Latyk's competitive recognition at a music festival in 2000. No exact date or festival location is stated on the visible award page, so neither is assigned in this archival record.

The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible document and are provided by the archive as identity context.

Notes

The inside award page directly identifies Уляна Латик and records her as a лауреат of «Різдвяні дзвіночки – 2000».

The document explicitly states III місце and середня група. The archival record preserves those terms as they appear on the source and does not reinterpret the award as a different competitive classification.

The document directly identifies the jury chair as А. Фіглюк. External biographical sources identify him as Анатолій Арсентійович Фіглюк, a poet, songwriter, journalist, and master of ceremonies. Biographical information published by the Virtual Museum of the Institute of Journalism states that nearly 150 songs have been written to his lyrics.

The accompanying handwritten signature of the jury chair is preserved as part of the physical source but is not independently interpreted in this archival record.

The year 2000 is directly contained in the festival name. No separate exact date is visible on the document.

An official stamp and an additional handwritten signature appear on the award page. The additional handwritten signature is not independently interpreted where it cannot be read with sufficient certainty.

No festival location or exact age range corresponding to the середня група is stated on the visible document. Neither is inferred in this archival record.

The standardized birth name Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk, the professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.
