Description

Винники Plus published the interview “Я не хворію на шоуманію і не хочу, щоб на мні зарбляли, — Ляна Новак (ВІДЕО)” on 28 October 2013. The publication features Ukrainian singer Lyana Novak and explicitly identifies her as «Ляна Новак (Уляна Латик)».

She was born Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk and later used the professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak. Her current legal name is Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and she currently performs under the stage name Ana Danch.

The interview discusses her music career, participation in television projects, music videos, repertoire and future artistic plans. Among the subjects mentioned are her participation in the casting for “Фабрика зірок-4”, her music videos, songs including “Не плач” and “Підманула”, and projects she was developing at that period of her career.

The introduction to the publication states that Lyana Novak gave the interview to Larysa Kubska (Лариса Кубська), a journalist of the newspaper «Винниківський Вісник».

Notes

The publication directly identifies Lyana Novak as Uliana Latyk through the wording «Ляна Новак (Уляна Латик)», providing documentary evidence linking the professional name used during this period with her birth name.

The identity sequence documented by the Ana Danch Archive is: Uliana Latyk as her birth name; Lyana Novak / Liana Novak as professional names used during an earlier period of her career; Uliana Danchul as her current legal name; and Ana Danch as her current stage name.

The publication title is preserved exactly as it appears on the source website, including spelling and typographical errors in the original heading.

The webpage displays the account name admin. This does not by itself establish individual authorship of the online publication, so the Ana Danch Archive does not assign an individual author without direct evidence.

The introduction identifies Larysa Kubska as a journalist of «Винниківський Вісник» to whom the interview was given. The source does not explicitly identify her as the author of the Vynnyky Plus web publication.
