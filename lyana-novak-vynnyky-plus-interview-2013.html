Description

This archival record documents a 2013 Винники Plus publication featuring Lyana Novak. The source explicitly identifies her as «Ляна Новак (Уляна Латик)». She was born Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk and later used the professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak. Her current legal name is Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and she currently performs under the stage name Ana Danch.

The interview covers her music career, participation in television projects, music videos, repertoire and future artistic plans. The introduction states that the interview was given to Larysa Kubska (Лариса Кубська), a journalist of the newspaper «Винниківський Вісник».

Archive significance: The publication provides direct documentary evidence connecting the professional name Lyana Novak with the birth name Uliana Latyk. Together with the verified biographical record preserved by the Ana Danch Archive, it documents the identity continuity from Uliana Latyk through the professional names Lyana Novak / Liana Novak to the current legal name Uliana Danchul and the current stage name Ana Danch.
