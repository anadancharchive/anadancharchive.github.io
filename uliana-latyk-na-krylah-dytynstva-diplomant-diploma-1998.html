Description

This diploma records ЛАТИК УЛЯНА as a ДИПЛОМАНТ of the festival «На крилах дитинства», identified on the document as a Фестиваль дитячої української естрадної пісні.

The front cover displays the year 98, providing direct documentary evidence that this archival item dates from 1998.

The document identifies О. Албул as the jury chair and О. Соколик as the organizing committee chair. Their abbreviated printed forms are preserved exactly as they appear on the physical source.

No exact calendar date or location is stated on the visible document.

The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible diploma and are provided by the archive as identity context.
