Description

J'imagine published the biographical article “Ana Danch : l’âme moderne d’un héritage folk ukrainien” on 6 August 2025. Written by Eric Cooper, the publication presents an extensive overview of Ana Danch’s artistic background and her contemporary interpretation of Ukrainian musical traditions.

The article identifies the artist as Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, née Latyk, born on 16 November 1987 in Lviv. It describes her early involvement with music, including beginning vocal studies at the age of seven, her first public performance at the age of nine, and participation in numerous national and international vocal competitions and festivals.

The publication also covers her education and earlier professional career, including her activity as lead vocalist of the Ukrainian girl group Dessert from 2005 to 2006 and Trishi from 2006 to 2007, as well as later work in television.

In a section devoted to her return to music, J'imagine states that after a long break she returned to the stage in 2021 under the stage name Ana Danch. This makes the publication an important biographical source directly connecting her birth name, legal name and current stage identity.

The article discusses Ana Danch’s contemporary work with Ukrainian folk material and highlights the song “Підманула”. At the time of publication, J'imagine presented the EP “Підманула” as scheduled for release on 26 September 2025 and described it as a five-track project based on modern interpretations of Ukrainian traditional songs.

The publication also refers to Ana Danch’s appearance on the Duff Radio Podcast in October 2024 and discusses her artistic activity during the war, the relationship between family and music, and her interest in international artistic collaboration.

Notes

The publication is particularly significant for the archive because it directly connects the names Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk, Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul and Ana Danch within a single biographical source.

Statements in the article concerning the popularity, reach or online performance of individual recordings reflect information presented by J'imagine in the original publication and are documented as source claims rather than independently established archival facts.

The article was published before the announced release date of the EP “Підманула”. References to the EP and its 26 September 2025 release date therefore reflect the information available in the publication at the time it appeared.
