Description

"Champagne (Lounge Version)" is a reimagined lounge interpretation of Ana Danch's original single "Champagne", transforming the energetic pop atmosphere of the original release into a refined, intimate listening experience. Built on smooth electronic textures, warm ambient harmonies and elegant lounge aesthetics, the arrangement reveals a more emotional and cinematic side of the composition.

Written by Uliana Danchul, the song preserves its sophisticated lyrical imagery while offering a completely different mood through relaxed rhythms and atmospheric production. Released digitally on 22 August 2024 via ANA DANCH and distributed worldwide by Symphonic Distribution.
