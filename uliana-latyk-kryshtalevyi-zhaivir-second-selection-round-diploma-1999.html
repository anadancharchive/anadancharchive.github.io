Description

This diploma records Латик Уляна as a participant in the II відбірковий тур of the children's song art festival «Кришталевий жайвір».

The document identifies the festival as a фестиваль дитячого пісенного мистецтва and records Тернопіль and the year 1999.

The diploma documents participation in the second selection round. It does not state a prize placement, laureate status, or victory.

The document itself records the recipient under the name Латик Уляна. The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible diploma.
