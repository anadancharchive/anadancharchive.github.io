Description

This diploma was awarded to ЛАТИК УЛЯНА as a laureate of the First Prize at the Всеукраїнський телевізійний фестиваль дитячого естрадного мистецтва «Азовські вітрила-2000».

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the early artistic career of the singer then performing under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The diploma records the recipient as ЛАТИК УЛЯНА. The standardized Latin-script form Uliana Latyk, the later professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document directly records Uliana Latyk as a laureate of the First Prize at the All-Ukrainian Television Festival of Children's Variety Art «Азовські вітрила-2000». No exact event or document date is stated on the visible diploma, so only the year 2000 is recorded in this archival entry.
