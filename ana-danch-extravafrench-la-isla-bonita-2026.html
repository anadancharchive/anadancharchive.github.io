Description

EXTRAVAFRENCH published the feature “Ana Danch s’offre une nuit sans retour sur « La Isla Bonita »” on 10 August 2026. The French-language article focuses on Ukrainian singer-songwriter Ana Danch and her single “La Isla Bonita”.

The publication describes “La Isla Bonita” as a song written in English and Spanish and built around themes of freedom, emotion and seaside memories. Rather than presenting an idealized summer postcard, the article emphasizes the feeling of temporary escape and the emotional significance of experiences that are understood to be fleeting.

EXTRAVAFRENCH characterizes the production as warm and dreamy indie pop, highlighting the melodies, the alternation between languages and the cinematic atmosphere of the recording. The article associates the song with Mediterranean imagery, including the sea, warmth and an extended summer night.

The publication also provides biographical context about Ana Danch. It identifies her as a Ukrainian singer from Lviv, states that she began studying vocals at the age of seven, first performed on stage at nine, participated in 59 national and international vocal competitions, and wrote her first original song at the age of fourteen.

The article further notes that, after experiences as a lead singer in music groups, she relaunched her artistic career under the name Ana Danch in 2021. It presents “La Isla Bonita” as another musical direction within a career developed since childhood rather than as an isolated summer release.
