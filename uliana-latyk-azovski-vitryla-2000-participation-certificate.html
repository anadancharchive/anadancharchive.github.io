Description

This participation certificate records ЛАТИК УЛЯНА as a participant in the Всеукраїнський телевізійний фестиваль дитячого естрадного мистецтва «АЗОВСЬКІ ВІТРИЛА-2000».

The document identifies Анатолій АВДІЄВСЬКИЙ as the head of the jury and describes him as a People's Artist of Ukraine, laureate of the Taras Shevchenko National Prize of Ukraine, and academician.

The document itself records the participant under the name ЛАТИК УЛЯНА. The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible certificate.

Notes

The certificate directly records ЛАТИК УЛЯНА as a participant in the Всеукраїнський телевізійний фестиваль дитячого естрадного мистецтва «АЗОВСЬКІ ВІТРИЛА-2000».

The standardized Latin-script birth name Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk, the professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

No exact date is stated on the visible certificate, so only the year 2000, appearing as part of the festival identification, is recorded in this archival entry.

The handwritten signature is not independently interpreted. The name and title of the head of the jury are recorded only from the printed information visible on the document.
