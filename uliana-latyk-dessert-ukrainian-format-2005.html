Description

This 2005 newspaper feature by Mariana Zbozhna documents an early and already successful period in the career of the Ukrainian female group Dessert (Десерт). The publication reports that Dessert, with the song “Так буває” (“Tak Buvaie”), had been the leader of the “Ukrainian Format” (“Український формат”) radio project in Lviv for two consecutive weeks, providing contemporary documentary evidence of the group’s early radio success.

The article is particularly significant because it clearly distinguishes the vocal roles of the two members. Uliana Latyk is identified as the soloist of Dessert, while Mariana Krylyk is identified as the group’s backing vocalist.

The publication further records that Dessert had existed for only two months but had already appeared on the stage of the Tavriyski Ihry (Таврійські ігри) festival. It documents that the group was initially called Karamel (Карамель) before adopting the name Dessert (Десерт), and describes how Uliana Latyk and Mariana Krylyk met and subsequently decided to form the duo. At the time of publication, the group was preparing its fifth song with an arranger who had previously worked with Gaitana and Iryna Bilyk.

The article is an important archival source for documenting the early development of Dessert, its recognition in Ukrainian radio, and the distinct performing roles of its members.
