Description

This preserved concert poster dated 30 March 2007 documents a performance by the Ukrainian group TRISHI (ТРИШИ) with the program «Лунная ночь» (Lunnaya Noch).

The poster features group members Uliana Latyk, Yuliia Andrushchenko and Karina Koia-Krasa (Каріна Койа-Краса). Uliana Latyk is now known professionally as Ana Danch.

The material documents a later TRISHI lineup in which Karina Koia-Krasa appears as a group member. The composition of the group changed during its period of activity, and this poster is preserved as evidence of that particular 2007 lineup.

The poster announces a performance beginning at 23:00 and includes the printed phrase «ЖИВОЙ ЗВУК». The venue name is printed as «ШТОРМ», together with the address Садовая, 4а and telephone number 46-46-77.

This item is documented as promotional material for TRISHI and is clearly distinguished from later solo material released under the Ana Danch stage name.
