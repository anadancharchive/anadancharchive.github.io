Description

This diploma records Латик Уляні as having been awarded the title of diplomant of the All-Ukrainian Children's Television Festival of Popular Song «Надія – 2001».

The diploma is dated 14 January 2002 and identifies the place as Львів.

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the early artistic career of the singer under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The diploma records the recipient as Латик Уляні. The standardized Latin-script form Uliana Latyk, the later professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document directly records that Uliana Latyk was awarded the title of diplomant of the All-Ukrainian Children's Television Festival of Popular Song «Надія – 2001». Although the festival title contains the year 2001, the visible diploma itself is dated 14 January 2002 in Lviv. The handwritten signature of the jury chair is not identified in this archival entry.
