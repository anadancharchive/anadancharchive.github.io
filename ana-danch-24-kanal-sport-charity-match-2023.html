Description

24 Kanal Sport published the article “Ветерани Карпат обіграли збірну журналістів в благодійному матчі на підтримку дітей-сиріт” on 14 October 2023. The publication reported on the annual charity football match “Кубок Львова”, held in Lviv on 13 October 2023.

The 2023 event was the fourth edition of the charity match. Its principal purpose was to support orphaned children and displaced children by helping provide opportunities for them to participate in sports.

The publication states that Ukrainian singer Ana Danch performed the National Anthem of Ukraine before the match, after which the opening whistle was given. The article therefore provides direct contemporary documentation of Ana Danch’s public performance in Lviv on 13 October 2023.

The charity initiative also raised funds toward the construction and development of a children's sports ground for Children's Home No. 1 in Lviv. The match featured veterans of FC Karpaty and a team of journalists.
