Description

"Не плач" is the first digital album by Ana Danch, released on 19 May 2024. The album represents a musical journey from Uliana Latyk, the name under which the singer began her early artistic career, to Ana Danch, the stage identity under which she returned to music and continued her work as a singer and songwriter.

Across ten tracks, the album brings together songs connected with different periods of her life and creative path. Each composition reflects particular emotions, experiences and stages in the artist's development, allowing the album to serve as a retrospective portrait of the musical journey that shaped Ana Danch.

"Не плач" connects earlier songs with later recordings and remix interpretations, bringing different periods of the artist's catalogue together in a single digital album. At the same time, it marks a transition between the music that formed her artistic identity and a new stage of creativity, opening the way for new songs, ideas and musical directions in the work of Ana Danch.
