Description

This preserved promotional card features Uliana Latyk, now known professionally as Ana Danch.

It represents a second preserved variant of promotional material from the same period and uses a different portrait from the separately archived promotional card.

The front side contains a monochrome portrait of Uliana Latyk with her name printed directly on the image as «Уляна Латик».

The reverse side preserves the printed credit «фото: OKS models / Андрій Гончаренко», telephone numbers for concert organization and a producer credit for Олег Климович.

No date or year is printed on either preserved side. The archive therefore does not assign a chronological date by assumption.

The material is preserved as part of the visual documentation of Ana Danch’s early artistic career under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The name Uliana Latyk represents the artist during an earlier period of her career. She is now known professionally as Ana Danch.

This archival record documents a separate promotional card variant with a different portrait from the other preserved Uliana Latyk promotional card.

The original material identifies the featured person directly by the printed name «Уляна Латик».

The reverse side reproduces the credit «фото: OKS models / Андрій Гончаренко» and identifies Олег Климович as producer.

Historical telephone numbers and printed contact information are reproduced as documentary evidence of the original promotional material. Their inclusion does not imply that the information remains current.

Because no date or year is visible on the preserved material, no date has been added by assumption.
