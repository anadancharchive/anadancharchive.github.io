Description

This certificate of appreciation records Ляна Новак as being thanked for participation in the charity concert «Вогонь сердець» and for supporting the creative development of talented children.

The document was issued by Громадська Організація «Вогонь Сердець» and is dated 17 April 2018.

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the artistic career of the singer during the period when she performed professionally under the name Lyana Novak.
