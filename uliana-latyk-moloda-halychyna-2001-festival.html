Description

On 12 April 2001, the Ukrainian newspaper Moloda Halychyna (Молода Галичина) published the article "Українська пісня: з «Надією» — сподіваймося!" ("Ukrainian Song: With 'Nadiya' — Let Us Hope!"), dedicated to the first round of the All-Ukrainian Children's Television Festival of Popular Music "Nadiya – 2001". The publication summarizes the results of the first selection round, presents the jury members, describes the goals of the festival, and highlights several young finalists. The article features a photograph of Uliana Latyk with the caption "Фіналіст фестивалю Уляна Латик" ("Festival finalist Uliana Latyk"), documenting her participation in the national television competition. This publication documents Uliana Latyk's participation in the All-Ukrainian Children's Television Festival of Popular Music "Nadiya – 2001" before adopting the stage name Ana Danch.
