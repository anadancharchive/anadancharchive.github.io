Description

Showbiza hosts an online artist biography profile under the name Liana Novak, with the alternative form Lyana Novak displayed alongside the profile name. The page lists the artist's birthday as 16 November 1987 and identifies Lviv as her birthplace.

The Russian-language biography documents an earlier period of the artist's career. It describes her participation in numerous music competitions and festivals, her work with the groups Dessert and TRISHI, and her subsequent solo career. The biography also refers to the songs and music videos “В ритме мира”, “Бали” and “Не плач”.

The profile is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as a historical record documenting the professional names Liana Novak and Lyana Novak used during an earlier period in the career of the artist now known professionally as Ana Danch.

Notes

The Showbiza profile documents an earlier period of the artist's career under the professional names Liana Novak and Lyana Novak. The profile predates her later legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul and current stage name Ana Danch; these later identity details are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as archival context.

The profile displays the distinction TOP3 – 2015. This confirms the profile's documented presence in the 2015 period, but it is not treated as an explicit publication date for the biography because no separate publication date is displayed on the source page.
