Description

This archival record documents a 2001 photo session with Uliana Latyk, who is now known professionally as Ana Danch.

The preserved material consists of seven photographs from the same photo session. The collection includes both color and monochrome photographs and is preserved as a single archival record rather than as seven separate photographic entries.

Photography is credited to Andrii Honcharenko (Андрій Гончаренко). Makeup is credited to Мар'яна Дідиченко. The archival information associated with the session also identifies OK'S fashion company.

The photographs document Uliana Latyk during the early period of her artistic career and form part of the photographic history preserved in the Ana Danch Archive.
