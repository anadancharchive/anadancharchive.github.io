Description

This diploma records Уляна Латик as a Дипломант of the All-Ukrainian Festival of Young Performers of Contemporary Ukrainian Song «Молода Галичина» in 2000.

The document identifies the festival year as 2000 and states the location м. Новояворівськ.

The diploma records diplomant status but does not state a specific prize placement.

The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible diploma and are provided by the archive as identity context.

Notes

The diploma directly identifies Уляна Латик as a Дипломант of the festival «Молода Галичина».

The document states the year 2000 and the location м. Новояворівськ. No exact date is stated on the visible diploma.

The document does not state a specific competitive placement or prize. No such placement is assigned in this archival entry.

Handwritten names and signatures in the jury section are not independently interpreted where they cannot be read with sufficient certainty from the physical document.

The standardized birth name Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk, the professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.
