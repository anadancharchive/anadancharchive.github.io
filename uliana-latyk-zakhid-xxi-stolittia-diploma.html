Description

This diploma records Латик Уляна as a laureate and recipient of the I Prize at the festival «Захід – XXI століття».

The document identifies М. Сливоцький as the chairman of the jury and Б. Сташків as the festival director.

The diploma is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as a record of an early stage in the artistic career of the singer, then known by the name Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The diploma records the recipient as Латик Уляна. The standardized Latin-script form Uliana Latyk, the later professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document directly records Uliana Latyk as a laureate and recipient of the I Prize at the festival «Захід – XXI століття». It identifies М. Сливоцький as the chairman of the jury and Б. Сташків as the festival director. No exact date, year, or place of the event is stated on the visible diploma, so these details are not added to this archival entry. The printed notice «ПП “Фоліо Плюс”, м. Харків, 2000 р.» at the bottom of the document relates to the production of the diploma form and is not treated as the date or place of the award.
