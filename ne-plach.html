Description

"Не плач" is an original song by Ana Danch about hope, emotional resilience and the strength to overcome life's most difficult moments. Written by Uliana Danchul with music composed by Andriy Bakun, the song combines contemporary pop with sincere lyrics that encourage listeners not to lose faith despite pain and uncertainty. Released as a digital single on 12 May 2024, the song combines contemporary pop with a deeply personal lyrical narrative about hope and inner strength.
