Na Doliu Maibutnoi Natsii IV Certificate of Merit – Uliana Latyk (2009)

Basic Information

Document Type Certificate of Merit
Name on Document Уляна Латик
Recognition за безкорисливу участь в благодійному марафоні «На долю майбутньої нації IV»
Occasion З нагоди шостої річниці від заснування ЛМГО «Золота молодь Винник»
Date 25 October 2009
Year 2009
Language Ukrainian

Description

This certificate of merit records Уляна Латик as being recognized for her selfless participation in the charity marathon «На долю майбутньої нації IV».

The document was issued on the occasion of the sixth anniversary of the founding of ЛМГО «Золота молодь Винник» and is dated 25 October 2009.

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the artistic career of the singer under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The document records the recipient as Уляна Латик. The standardized Latin-script form Uliana Latyk, the later professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document directly records Uliana Latyk as being recognized for her selfless participation in the charity marathon «На долю майбутньої нації IV». It states that the certificate was issued on the occasion of the sixth anniversary of the founding of ЛМГО «Золота молодь Винник» and is dated 25 October 2009. The abbreviations ЛМГО and ЛМР are preserved as they appear on the document.
