Description

This physical award trophy bears the inscription «Люди Львова Золотий лев-2011», identifies журнал «RIA-Львів», and records the nomination «Співачка року».

No recipient name is printed on the visible trophy.

The trophy is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as a separate physical artifact connected with the same Zolotyi Lev-2011 Singer of the Year recognition already documented in the archive by an award plaque naming Ляна Новак.

Notes

The trophy directly records the award «Люди Львова Золотий лев-2011», the issuing publication журнал «RIA-Львів», and the nomination «Співачка року».

The recipient's name is not printed on the visible trophy. The association of this physical object with Lyana Novak is established in the Ana Danch Archive through the separately preserved award plaque for the same recognition, which directly names Ляна Новак as the recipient.

The standardized professional forms Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the birth name Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

This trophy and the previously archived award plaque are preserved as separate physical archival objects documenting the same 2011 award recognition.
