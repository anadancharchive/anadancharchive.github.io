Description

This commendation certificate was issued to Латик Уляні Мирославівні, a pupil of class 3-В at середня школа № 29 in м. Львова.

The document recognizes her «за відмінні успіхи у навчанні» and is dated 24 May 1997.

The document is significant as an early biographical record because it directly records the recipient's full name, including patronymic, as Латик Уляні Мирославівні, as well as her school, class, city, and the exact date of issue.

The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible document and are provided by the archive as identity context.

Notes

The document directly records the recipient as Латик Уляні Мирославівні. This provides a primary archival record of the full Ukrainian form of her birth name, including the patronymic Мирославівна.

The document identifies the recipient as a pupil of 3-В класу of середньої школи № 29 in м. Львова and states that the commendation was awarded за відмінні успіхи у навчанні.

The document bears the number 1266 and the exact date 24 травня 1997 р.. These details are recorded directly from the physical source.

A school stamp, a director's signature, and handwritten information associated with the signature appear on the document. The signature and handwritten name are not independently interpreted in this archival record.

This document concerns Uliana Latyk's school education rather than her musical activity. It is preserved as an early biographical and identity record within the Ana Danch Archive.
