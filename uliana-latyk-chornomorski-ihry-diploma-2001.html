Description

This diploma records Латик Уляна as a diplomant of the Fourth All-Ukrainian Charitable Children's Festival «Чорноморські ігри» held in Скадовськ in 2001.

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the early artistic career of the singer under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The diploma records the recipient as Латик Уляна. The standardized Latin-script form Uliana Latyk, the later professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document directly records Uliana Latyk as a diplomant of the Fourth All-Ukrainian Charitable Children's Festival «Чорноморські ігри» in Skadovsk in 2001. No exact date is stated on the visible diploma, so only the year 2001 is recorded in this archival entry.
