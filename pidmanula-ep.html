Description

"Підманула" (Pidmanula) is a five-track folk EP by Ana Danch, released on 26 September 2025. The project presents contemporary interpretations of traditional Ukrainian folk songs, combining recognizable folk material with modern arrangements, updated tempos and adapted lyrics.

The EP developed around the title track "Підманула" and brings together five recordings that approach Ukrainian folk music as a living tradition rather than a fixed historical form. Lively rhythms, humorous narratives and contemporary production connect the songs while preserving their traditional character.

Across the release, Ana Danch combines Ukrainian folklore with her own approach to performance and lyrical adaptation, creating a bright and energetic folk project intended for a contemporary audience.
