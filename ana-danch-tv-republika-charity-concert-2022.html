Description

Telewizja Republika published the article “TYLKO U NAS! Orkiestra Reprezentacyjna WP, gwiazdy muzycznych scen” on 10 May 2022. The publication announced a special charity concert for Ukraine scheduled for 12 May 2022 at 19:00, organized in connection with the ninth anniversary of Telewizja Republika.

The broadcaster presented the concert as an expression of support for Ukraine during the war. The programme included performances by the Representative Orchestra of the Polish Armed Forces and invited Ukrainian performers.

Among the announced guests was Ana Danch. The publication described her as a refugee and vocalist from Ukraine, documenting her artistic activity in Poland during the first months following Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine.

The article also announced the participation of Ukrainian child performer Amelka Anisovych, who had become internationally known for singing while sheltering in a bunker in Ukraine.

The concert was scheduled for live broadcast on Telewizja Republika, the broadcaster’s website and its Facebook profile on 12 May 2022 at 19:00. Additional repeat broadcasts were also announced.
