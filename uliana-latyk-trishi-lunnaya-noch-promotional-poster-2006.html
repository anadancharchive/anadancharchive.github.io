Description

This preserved promotional poster from 2006 documents the Ukrainian group TRISHI (ТРИШИ) and promotes the release «Лунная ночь» (Lunnaya Noch).

The poster features the three members of TRISHI: Uliana Latyk, Nataliia Stohnii and Yuliia Andrushchenko. Uliana Latyk is positioned in the center, between the other two members of the group.

Uliana Latyk is now known professionally as Ana Danch. The poster is preserved as part of the visual documentation of her early artistic career during her period as a member of TRISHI.

The upper section of the poster contains the printed promotional headline «ОТКРЫТИЕ ГОДА! НОВЫЙ УКРАИНСКИЙ ПРОЕКТ!». The design combines a group photograph with an image associated with the «Лунная ночь» release and printed promotional and distribution information.

This material is documented as a promotional item for TRISHI and is clearly distinguished from later solo material released under the Ana Danch stage name.
