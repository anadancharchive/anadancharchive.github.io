Description

MusicBrainz maintains a structured artist profile for Ana Danch, identified by the MusicBrainz Artist ID a791aaee-2905-4459-aaf2-761128bf24fe. The profile describes Ana Danch as a Ukrainian singer and songwriter and classifies the entity as a Person.

The MusicBrainz profile displays the legal-name field as “Uliana Danchul, Uliana Latyk”, directly associating both names with the Ana Danch artist entity. The page itself does not distinguish within that field which name represents the current legal name and which represents the birth name.

The Artist Information section records the sort name “Danch, Ana”, gender Female, date of birth 16 November 1987, birthplace Lviv, Lviv Oblast, Ukraine, and area Ukraine.

The profile also records two IPI codes: 01246012691 and 01246013296.

MusicBrainz displays genre tags including alternative pop, dream pop, electronic, folk and indie pop, together with the additional tags ethno, songwriter and ukrainian.

The profile contains a structured discography organized into albums, EP and compilation releases, and singles. The visible MusicBrainz catalogue includes releases from 2021 through 2026 and is explicitly presented by MusicBrainz as official release groups by the artist.

The External Links section connects the artist entity with the official Ana Danch Archive, Instagram, YouTube, SoundCloud, Facebook, Apple Music, Bandcamp, Deezer, Spotify, TikTok and Wikidata.

The source page does not display the professional names Lyana Novak or Liana Novak. These names are included in the Archive Information section as part of the independently documented identity history maintained by the Ana Danch Archive.

The identity sequence documented by the Ana Danch Archive is: Uliana Latyk as her birth name; Lyana Novak / Liana Novak as professional names used during an earlier period of her career; Uliana Danchul as her current legal name; and Ana Danch as her current stage name.

The release groups visible on the preserved profile therefore represent the state of the MusicBrainz database at the time of archiving and should not be interpreted as a complete catalogue of Ana Danch releases.
