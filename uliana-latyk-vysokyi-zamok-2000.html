Description

On 3 October 2000, the Ukrainian newspaper Vysokyi Zamok (Високий Замок) published an interview with Uliana Latyk titled "Одним гонораром я покрию всі батьківські витрати" ("With One Performance Fee I Will Cover All My Parents’ Expenses"). The interview documents another important stage in the early artistic career of Uliana Latyk, who at the age of thirteen was already actively participating in vocal competitions and festival performances throughout Ukraine. The article discusses her musical education, participation in artistic events, first earnings from performances, family support, creative aspirations, and future plans. Today, this publication remains another early press publication documenting the beginning of the public artistic career of Uliana Latyk before adopting the stage name Ana Danch.
