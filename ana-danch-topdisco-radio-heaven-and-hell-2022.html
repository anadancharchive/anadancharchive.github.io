Description

Topdisco Radio published the feature “Disco de la Semana (08) | Ana Danch - Heaven and Hell” on 24 February 2022. Written by Xavi Tobaja, the publication selected Ana Danch’s recording “Heaven and Hell” as Topdisco Radio’s featured “Disco de la Semana” for week 8 of 2022.

The publication describes “Heaven and Hell” as a cover of a song associated with C.C. Catch and identifies Dieter Bohlen as its composer.

The article also provides a short biographical introduction to the artist under her legal name Uliana Danchul, identifying her as a Ukrainian singer and songwriter born in Lviv. It refers to her participation in Ukrainian and international music competitions and festivals, her previous activity as a lead vocalist in Ukrainian music groups, and her return to creative activity after a long break.

The publication states that Uliana Danchul would continue her musical career under the stage name Ana Danch, making the article an important early international media source directly connecting her legal name with the stage name used for the new period of her artistic career.

Notes

The publication date, 24 February 2022, coincides with the beginning of Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine. According to the archival context of the Ana Danch project, the outbreak of the full-scale war significantly affected the beginning and subsequent development of the singer’s creative activity under the stage name Ana Danch.

The description of “Heaven and Hell” as a cover associated with C.C. Catch and the identification of Dieter Bohlen as composer reflect the information stated by Topdisco Radio in the original publication.
