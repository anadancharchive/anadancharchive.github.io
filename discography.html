Discography

The Discography section documents the musical career of Ana Danch, including her official solo releases and selected recordings from her early artistic career under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Uliana Latyk is now known professionally as Ana Danch. Group recordings from the earlier period of her career are preserved in a separate archival section in order to document the continuity of her artistic history without presenting those recordings as solo Ana Danch releases.

The archive documents albums, extended plays (EPs), singles, collaborative releases, cover recordings, and other released works. Each entry is presented as an individual archival record with verified discographic information.

Early Career – Group Recordings

This section documents recordings from Ana Danch’s early artistic career under her birth name, Uliana Latyk, including physical releases and compilation appearances created during her membership in musical groups.

These materials form part of the documented artistic history of Ana Danch. They are preserved as evidence of her earlier professional activity while remaining clearly distinguished from the solo Ana Danch release catalogue.
