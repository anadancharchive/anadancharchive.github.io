Description

Skarzysko24.pl published the article “Radość i zabawa na polsko - ukraińskim pikniku integracyjnym w Skarżysku” on 19 September 2022. The publication reported on a Polish-Ukrainian integration picnic held on 18 September 2022 in Skarżysko-Kamienna.

The event was organized as part of the “Świętokrzyskie dla Ukrainy” project and was intended to strengthen social connections between local residents and refugees from Ukraine staying in the area.

The organizer of the Polish-Ukrainian integration picnic was Starostwo Powiatowe w Skarżysku-Kamiennej. The programme included activities for children, community attractions and stage performances involving local participants and guests from Ukraine.

The publication states that ANA DANCH performed at the conclusion of the picnic and identifies her as a well-known Ukrainian vocalist. The article therefore provides direct contemporary documentation of Ana Danch’s live artistic activity in Poland in September 2022.
