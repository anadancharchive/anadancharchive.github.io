Description

This diploma records Uli Latyk, identified on the document as being from Lviv, for participation in the XIV Ogólnopolski Festiwal «Nowa Piosenka w Starym Krakowie».

The document records the festival in Kraków on 20–23 April 2001.

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the early artistic career of the singer under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The diploma records the recipient as Uli Latyk and identifies her as being from Lviv. The standardized form Uliana Latyk, the later professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document directly records participation in the XIV Ogólnopolski Festiwal «Nowa Piosenka w Starym Krakowie» in Kraków on 20–23 April 2001. The handwritten jury signatures are not identified in this archival entry.
