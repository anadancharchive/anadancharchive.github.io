Description

The EP «Магія» (Magic) is a story of love, mythology, and Ukrainian folklore, in which ancient imagery is given new life through a contemporary electronic sound.

The five tracks come together to form a single story. «Квітка» (Flower) immerses the listener in the atmosphere of Ivan Kupala and ancient Slavic beliefs. «Мавка» (Mavka) takes the listener into the world of Slavic mythology. «Магія» (Magic) explores a mysterious connection between two people that cannot be explained in words. «Ой, ти родися» (Oh, Grow) draws on Ukrainian folk imagery, exploring themes of love, one’s native land, roots, and a sense of belonging to one’s country. «Хто я?» (Who Am I?) brings the journey to a close, turning inward toward self-reflection.

The EP «Магія» (Magic) creates a world in which love, nature, mythology, and human emotion intertwine to form one cohesive story.
