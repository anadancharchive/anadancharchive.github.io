Description

"Квітка" ("Kvitka") is an original electronic-folk composition by Ana Danch, inspired by the ancient Slavic midsummer celebration of Ivan Kupala. The central image of the song is a mythical flower that, according to legend, blooms for only a single moment on the shortest night of the year. It symbolizes rare happiness and true love — something that cannot be forced, only felt when the right moment arrives.

"Квітка" ("Kvitka") tells the story of an encounter that opens a deeper understanding of oneself and the surrounding world. Music and lyrics were written by Uliana Danchul. The single was released digitally on 6 March 2026 via ANA DANCH and distributed worldwide by Symphonic Distribution.
