Description

This preserved promotional poster from summer 2006 documents the Ukrainian group TRISHI (ТРИШИ).

The poster features Uliana Latyk, Yuliia Andrushchenko and Karina Koia-Krasa (Каріна Койа-Краса). Uliana Latyk is now known professionally as Ana Danch.

The material documents a specific TRISHI lineup represented in promotional material from summer 2006. The membership of the group changed during its period of activity, including changes within the same year, so this archival record is limited to the lineup represented in this particular source.

The poster contains the printed designation «Группа «Триши»», a telephone number for concert organization and the group website www.trishy.net.

This item is documented as promotional material for TRISHI and is clearly distinguished from later solo material released under the Ana Danch stage name.
