Description

"Половинка (Anton FreeON Remix)" is a Slap House reinterpretation of Ana Danch's song "Половинка". Based on the original song written and composed by Andriy Bakun, the remix features additional music by Anton FreeON and presents the composition in a new electronic arrangement while preserving its emotional story about love, devotion and two people finding completeness in each other. Released as a digital single on 19 February 2024, the track offers an alternative interpretation of the original composition.
