Description

In 2004, an unidentified Ukrainian newspaper published an interview by Lidiia Melnyk with Uliana Latyk titled "Я перестала боятися власного голосу" ("I Stopped Being Afraid of My Own Voice"). The interview documents an important stage in the early professional career of Uliana Latyk following her admission to the Kyiv National University of Culture and Arts. The article discusses her musical education, participation in Martyn Choir (Мартин хор), performances at festivals and competitions, collaboration with composer Andrii Bakun, work on her debut album, original songs "Ne Plach" ("Don't Cry") and "Ty Vtratyv" ("You Lost"), television appearances, and her involvement in the contemporary Lviv music scene alongside young performers including Andrii Kniaz, Lilia Varvyn, and Andrii Zalisko. The interview also outlines her artistic plans, including the possibility of participating in the Ukrainian selection for the Eurovision Song Contest 2005. Today, this publication remains an important archival source documenting the early professional development of Uliana Latyk before adopting the stage name Ana Danch.
