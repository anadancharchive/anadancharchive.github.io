Description

This diploma records Латик Ульяна as a Дипломант of the Всеукраїнський конкурс молодих виконавців associated with the festival «Володимир-99».

The document identifies the location as Володимир-Волинський and records the year 1999.

The diploma documents the recipient's status as a diplomant. It does not state a specific competitive placement, prize, or laureate status, and none is inferred in this archival record.

The front of the document contains the commemorative wording «800 ліття Волинсько-Галицької держави» and the years 1199–1999. This wording is preserved as historical context of the physical document and is not treated as part of the formal festival title.

The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible diploma and are provided by the archive as identity context.

Notes

The award page directly records the recipient as Латик Ульяна. The spelling with ь in Ульяна is preserved exactly as it appears on the physical source.

The document directly records the status Дипломант. It does not specify first, second, third, or any other competitive placement, and no prize placement is assigned in this archival record.

The document identifies the event as Всеукраїнський конкурс молодих виконавців, the festival as «Володимир-99», and the location as Володимир-Волинський.

The front of the diploma bears the commemorative inscription «800 ліття Волинсько-Галицької держави» together with the years 1199–1999. This inscription is documented separately from the festival and competition titles.

The document contains printed jury-related fields together with handwritten names and signatures. Handwritten names and signatures are not independently interpreted where they cannot be read with sufficient certainty from the physical source.

No exact calendar date is stated on the visible diploma, so only the year 1999 is recorded.

The standardized birth name Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk, the professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.
