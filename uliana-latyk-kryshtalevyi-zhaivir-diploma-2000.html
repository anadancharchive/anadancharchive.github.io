Description

This diploma was awarded to Латик Уляна as a laureate of the Third Prize in the 10–14 years age category at the Всеукраїнський фестиваль-конкурс дитячого та юнацького пісенного мистецтва «Кришталевий жайвір» in Тернопіль in 2000.

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the early artistic career of the singer under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The diploma records the recipient as Латик Уляна. The standardized Latin-script form Uliana Latyk, the later professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document directly records Uliana Latyk as a laureate of the Third Prize in the 10–14 years age category at the All-Ukrainian Festival-Competition of Children's and Youth Song Art «Кришталевий жайвір» in Ternopil in 2000. No exact date is stated on the visible document, so only the year 2000 is recorded in this archival entry.
