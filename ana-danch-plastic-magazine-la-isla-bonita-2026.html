Description

Plastic Magazine published the feature “Indie Pop from Ana Danch” on 27 July 2026. Written by Damo Howard, the publication presents Ukrainian singer-songwriter Ana Danch and focuses on her single “La Isla Bonita”.

The article places the release within the wider context of Ana Danch’s relationship with music and her development as a recording artist before turning its attention to “La Isla Bonita”.

Plastic Magazine describes “La Isla Bonita” as an original English-Spanish indie pop song. The publication highlights the combination of English and Spanish lyrics as an important element of the recording’s character and identity.

The article presents the song through themes of freedom, escape and unforgettable summer nights, describing a setting in which everyday life temporarily disappears and gives way to emotion, adventure and personal connection.
