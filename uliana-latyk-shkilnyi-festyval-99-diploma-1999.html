Description

This diploma was awarded to Латик Уляна on 21 November 1999 for I place in the vocal category at the IV Festival of Children's Creativity «Шкільний фестиваль-99» held in Жидачів.

The document is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as an archival record of the early artistic career of the singer then performing under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Notes

The diploma records the recipient as Латик Уляна. The standardized Latin-script form Uliana Latyk, the later professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The document directly records the award of first place in the vocal category at the IV Festival of Children's Creativity «Шкільний фестиваль-99» on 21 November 1999.
