Description

This archival record documents a publication by VIVA.ua about Ukrainian artist Ana Danch and her EP «Магія».

The publication describes «Магія» as a five-track musical story combining love, Slavic mythology, Ukrainian folklore, and the search for personal identity. The EP moves from images of nature and ancient beliefs to personal questions about oneself.

The article discusses the five compositions: «Квітка», which evokes Ivan Kupala traditions and Slavic beliefs; «Мавка», which continues the mythological theme through imagery from Ukrainian cultural tradition; the title track «Магія», about an inexplicable connection between two people; «Ой, ти родися», which addresses love, one's homeland, roots, and belonging to one's country; and «Хто я?», which focuses on personal doubts, life's path, and self-understanding.

The publication notes that Ana Danch combines Ukrainian cultural symbolism with contemporary electronic music, using ancient motifs as part of a modern sound.
