Description

"Підманула" is Ana Danch's contemporary interpretation of a traditional Ukrainian folk song. Released digitally on 12 May 2022, the recording preserves the recognizable folk character of the source material while presenting it through a modern musical arrangement.

The song combines traditional Ukrainian folklore with adapted lyrics associated with Uliana Danchul, giving the familiar folk narrative a renewed and playful character while retaining its connection to Ukrainian musical tradition.

With its lively rhythm and humorous lyrical character, "Підманула" represents Ana Danch's approach to folk material: preserving the identity of the traditional song while reworking it for a contemporary audience.
