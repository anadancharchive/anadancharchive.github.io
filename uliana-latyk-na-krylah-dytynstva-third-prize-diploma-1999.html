Description

This diploma records Латик Уляна as a лауреат III премії of the festival «На крилах дитинства», identified on the document as a Фестиваль дитячої української естрадної пісні.

The award page directly associates Uliana Latyk with the Вокально-хорова студія «Хорал» and the Винниківський Будинок школяра. This provides a primary archival record of her early musical affiliation in 1999.

The document is dated 9 травня 1999 р. and identifies the location as м. Винники.

The diploma identifies the jury chair as Мар'ян Шуневич and prints his title as Заслужений артист України. External biographical information from the Lviv National Philharmonic identifies him as Ukrainian estrada singer Мар'ян Васильович Шуневич.

The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible diploma and are provided by the archive as identity context.

Notes

The diploma directly records Латик Уляна as a лауреат III премії of the festival «На крилах дитинства».

The front of the document identifies the event as a Фестиваль дитячої української естрадної пісні.

The award page directly records Вокально-хорова студія «Хорал» and Винниківський Будинок школяра beneath the recipient's name. These details are preserved as direct evidence of Uliana Latyk's musical affiliation at the time of the award.

The document bears the exact date 9 травня 1999 р. and the location м. Винники. The exact date is therefore recorded as 1999-05-09 in the structured metadata of this archival page.

The document identifies the jury chair as Мар'ян Шуневич and gives the title Заслужений артист України. The Lviv National Philharmonic identifies him as Мар'ян Васильович Шуневич, a Ukrainian estrada singer, and records his merited artist title from 1989.

The jury chair's handwritten signature and the other handwritten names and signatures appearing on the document are preserved as part of the physical source. They are not independently interpreted beyond information that can be identified with sufficient certainty.

The standardized birth name Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk, the professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.
