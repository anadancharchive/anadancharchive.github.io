Description

Ukrainian Soul is a deeply personal and conceptually cohesive album by Ana Danch, bringing together Ukrainian musical tradition and a contemporary sound. Work on the project began in 2021, but the full-scale war in Ukraine affected both the timeline of its completion and the emotional context in which the album was created.

The album seeks to introduce the richness of Ukrainian culture to an international audience through romantic, profound and sincere songs that reflect the spirit of the Ukrainian people. It features a carefully selected repertoire, with each composition carrying its own emotional and cultural significance. Some lyrics were thoughtfully adapted, while new arrangements were created to give the songs a contemporary sonic identity while preserving their character.

With Ukrainian Soul, Ana Danch reinterprets Ukrainian musical heritage through her own artistic perspective while seeking to preserve and carry it forward for future generations. The album combines contemporary production with respect for the authenticity, emotional depth and enduring soul of Ukrainian folk music.
