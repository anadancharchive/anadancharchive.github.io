Description

This archival record documents the Ana Danch studio photo session held on 23 September 2025, featuring Ukrainian singer and songwriter Ana Danch, legally known as Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul (Уляна Мирославівна Данчул).

The photo session was photographed by Oleksandr Danchul (Олександр Данчул) at NLstudio (NL photostudio Lviv).

Oleksandr Serhiyovych Danchul (Олександр Сергійович Данчул) is separately credited as the producer of Ana Danch. His role as producer and his role as photographer of this photo session are documented separately.

Fifteen photographs from the session are preserved in the Ana Danch Archive and are presented together as one archival photographic record.
