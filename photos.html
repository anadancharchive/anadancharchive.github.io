Photos

The Photos section documents the artistic career of Ana Danch through archival photographs and visual materials. Images documenting her early artistic career may appear under her birth name, Uliana Latyk.

Contents

The archive documents artist portraits, photo sessions, live performances, promotional photographs, promotional pocket calendars, behind-the-scenes photographs, and other visual materials related to the artistic career of Ana Danch. Each item is presented as an individual archival record with descriptive information and available source details.
