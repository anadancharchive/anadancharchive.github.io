Description

This diploma records Ульяна Латик as a participant in the хіт-парад III відбіркового туру of the All-Ukrainian festival-competition of children's and youth song art «Кришталевий жайвір».

The document records the year 2000 and identifies the location as м. Тернопіль.

The diploma documents participation in the hit parade of the third selection round. It does not state a prize placement, laureate status, or victory.

The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible diploma and are provided by the archive as identity context.

Notes

The diploma directly records Ульяна Латик as a participant in the хіт-парад III відбіркового туру of the festival «Кришталевий жайвір».

The document states the year 2000 and the location м. Тернопіль. No exact date is stated on the visible diploma.

The document does not state a competitive placement, prize, or laureate status. No such status is assigned in this archival entry.

The printed positions Голова журі and Голова Оргкомітету appear on the diploma. The accompanying handwritten names and signatures are not independently interpreted in this archival record.

This document is preserved separately from other Kryshtalevyi Zhaivir diplomas documenting different festival stages and recognitions.

The standardized birth name Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk, the professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.
