Description

This diploma records Ульяна Латик as a Лауреат III-ї премії of the All-Ukrainian festival-competition «Вечори над Латорицею».

The document bears the date 17 вересня 2000 р. and identifies Мукачево at the bottom of the diploma. The recipient entry also includes м. Львів.

The diploma directly records the third-prize laureate status and identifies the diploma as III ступеню.

The professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch do not appear on the visible diploma and are provided by the archive as identity context.

Notes

The diploma directly identifies Ульяна Латик as a Лауреат III-ї премії of the festival-competition «Вечори над Латорицею».

The exact date 17 September 2000 is handwritten on the document. The diploma also states Мукачево 2000 at the bottom.

The entry м. Львів appears beside the recipient's name and is recorded separately from the festival location shown as Мукачево.

The printed positions Голова оргкомітету, Директор фестивалю, and Голова журі appear on the diploma. The accompanying signatures are not independently interpreted in this archival record.

The standardized birth name Uliana Myroslavivna Latyk, the professional names Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, the current legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul, and the current stage name Ana Danch are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.
