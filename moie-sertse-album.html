Description

"Моє серце" (My Heart) takes its title from the song co-written by Ana Danch and Anatolii Figliuk, a well-known poet and songwriter and a longtime friend of her family.

The album has a cohesive sound throughout, with all tracks arranged and produced by Kharkiv-based musician Yevhenii Rukonyatov, while the vocals for the entire album were recorded by Mykhailo Bushko.

The album also features two songs that Ana Danch performed during her childhood. "Дощ" (Rain) and "Вітер надій" (Wind of Hope) were revisited and newly recorded, giving these early songs a renewed life.

The album also includes the romantic compositions "Небокрай" (Nebokrai) and "Цілував" (Tsiluvav), the light and dance-oriented "Маямі" (Miami), and "Осіння ніч" (Autumn Night), a song in the dark pop style.
