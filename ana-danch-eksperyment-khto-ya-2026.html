Description

This archival record documents an article published by Експеримент on 18 September 2026 about Ukrainian singer and songwriter Ana Danch and her single «Хто я?».

According to the publication, Ana Danch released «Хто я?» on 17 September 2026. The article describes the song as an author’s composition about the search for personal identity, one’s own path, and the questions a person may ask at a certain point in life.

The publication describes «Хто я?» as a song built around questions about who we are, where we are going, and whether the chosen path corresponds to our true desires. The article states that the song does not offer ready-made answers, leaving these questions open to the listener.

The article identifies «Хто я?» as the final composition of the EP «Магія», whose release was scheduled for 30 September 2026. According to the publication, the EP contains five songs combining contemporary music with Ukrainian cultural imagery.

The publication describes the other compositions of the EP through references to Ukrainian mythology, Ivan Kupala traditions, love, Ukrainian cultural roots and belonging. In the final song, the focus shifts from external images to the person herself and the question of identity and personal path.

The article identifies Ana Danch as the author of both the music and lyrics of «Хто я?».
