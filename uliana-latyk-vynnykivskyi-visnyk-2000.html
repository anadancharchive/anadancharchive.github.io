Description

On 14 September 2000, the Ukrainian newspaper Vynnykivskyi Visnyk (Винниківський Вісник) published an interview with Uliana Latyk titled «Дитинство залишилося далеко позаду...» (“Childhood Has Been Left Far Behind…”).

The interview documents a stage in Uliana Latyk’s early artistic career, when she was performing as a young vocalist and taking part in musical events and vocal competitions in Ukraine.

The article discusses her childhood, first stage appearances, education, family support, musical achievements, and future ambitions. One of the accompanying photographs features Uliana Latyk together with Ukrainian singer Oleksandr Ponomariov.

This archival record identifies the publication by its newspaper title, issue number, publication date, and page reference. The original article is preserved in the archive through images of the newspaper and interview page.
