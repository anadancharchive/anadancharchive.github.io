Description

On 9 June 2010, Ukrainian newspaper Високий Замок published an interview with Ляна Новак by journalist Галина Гузьо.

The publication directly connects the artist's professional name Ляна Новак with her earlier identity as Уляна Латик. The subtitle describes her as a singer known to audiences in Galicia under the name Uliana Latyk, while the article states that she had returned from the Kyiv show-business environment to Lviv under the new name Lyana Novak.

The interview reviews her earlier musical career, including childhood singing competitions, participation in «Мартин-хор», the group Dessert and the girl group TRISHI. It also discusses her departure from TRISHI, a later invitation from producer Yurii Nikitin to join the group «Нікіта», her return to Lviv and her plans to continue her solo music career.

The publication is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as independent contemporary documentation linking the birth name Uliana Latyk with the professional name Lyana Novak during an earlier period in the career of the artist now known professionally as Ana Danch.

Notes

The original publication uses the Ukrainian professional name Ляна Новак and explicitly identifies her earlier name as Уляна Латик. The Latin-script forms Lyana Novak and Liana Novak, as well as the later legal name Uliana Myroslavivna Danchul and current stage name Ana Danch, are provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context.

The article is particularly significant as a contemporary independent source directly documenting the transition from the name Uliana Latyk to the professional identity Lyana Novak.
