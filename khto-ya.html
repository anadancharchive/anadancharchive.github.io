Description

"Хто я?" ("Who Am I?") is an author's song by Ana Danch, written and composed by Uliana Danchul. The song is about the search for oneself, finding one's own path, and confronting the questions we ask both fate and ourselves. Its honest and personal lyrics are complemented by a distinctive electronic sound.
