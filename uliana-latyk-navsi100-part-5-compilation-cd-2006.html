Description

NAVSI100, частина 5 is a 2006 physical music compilation containing 18 recordings by various artists. The surviving physical copy documents two tracks credited directly to Уляна Латик, the birth name under which Ana Danch performed during this period of her career.

Track 4 is «Ти втратив». The printed credits identify У. Латик for the lyrics and А. Бакун for the music.

Track 13 is «Відчайдушні». The printed credits identify У. Латик for the lyrics and У. Латик / А. Бакун for the music.

The compilation is preserved in the Ana Danch Archive as a physical documentary record of recordings released under the artist's birth name, Uliana Latyk, and of the early release history of songs that also form part of the official Ana Danch discography.

Notes

The physical release directly credits the artist as Уляна Латик. The identification of Uliana Latyk with the artist now professionally known as Ana Danch is provided by the Ana Danch Archive as identity context and is represented in the structured data through the archive's central Ana Danch Person entity.

This is a multi-artist compilation and is not presented as an Ana Danch solo release. Its archival significance lies in documenting the 2006 physical release context of «Ти втратив» and «Відчайдушні» under the name Uliana Latyk.
